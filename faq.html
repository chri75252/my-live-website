FAQ

Clear answers about the company and website.

Find the main information about products, supplier opportunities, The Forge and how to get in touch.

What is The Blacksmith Market?

The Blacksmith Market Ltd is a UK-registered company exploring selected product opportunities, supplier relationships and practical developments in modern commerce.

Does the website currently sell products or services?

The site currently provides company, supplier and editorial information. Any live commercial offering will be clearly described with its scope and terms when available.

Which product categories are considered?

Selected categories include beauty and personal care, home and living, toys and leisure, consumer technology and general merchandise. Interest varies by opportunity.

What should a supplier send?

Send enough information to identify the product and assess price, quantity, pack configuration, availability, delivery and relevant documentation. The Sell to Us page contains the full list.

Does submitting a range guarantee a purchase?

No. Submission starts an initial review only and does not create an obligation to purchase, list or distribute stock.

What is The Forge?

The Forge is The Blacksmith Market’s Blog & Resources section, covering AI and automation, ecommerce, digital retail, emerging technology, wholesale and B2B commerce.

How are articles prepared?

Articles should use current sources, clear attribution and practical analysis. Material updates are dated, and commercial relationships that affect coverage should be disclosed.

How can I contact the company?

Use the Contact page or email info@theblacksmithmarket.com.
