How The Forge prepares and updates content.

A concise standard for evidence, original value, authorship, AI assistance, disclosures and corrections.

Purpose

The Forge publishes practical material about AI and automation, ecommerce, online business, digital retail, technology, wholesale and B2B commerce.

Sources and evidence

Changeable technical and platform claims should use current primary or authoritative sources. Platform statistics are described as publisher-reported unless supported by independent evidence. Interpretation should be distinguishable from fact.

Original value

Articles should add a framework, comparison, checklist, model, test, worked example or other practical value rather than merely rewrite existing material.

Authorship and publisher

Content is published by The Blacksmith Market Ltd until a genuine individual author profile is approved. A name or biography must not be invented to imply experience or company scale.

AI assistance

AI tools may assist research organisation, drafting, editing, data transformation or technical checks. Published claims remain the responsibility of The Blacksmith Market and should be checked against cited or directly held evidence.

Commercial disclosure

Sponsorship, affiliate arrangements, supplied access or another commercial relationship that could affect coverage should be disclosed on the relevant page.

Corrections and updates

Material corrections should update the article and modified date. Requests can be sent to info@theblacksmithmarket.com with the page URL and supporting information.
