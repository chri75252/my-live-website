Privacy notice

This notice explains how information connected with website visits, enquiries and potential commercial relationships is handled.

1. Who we are

The Blacksmith Market Ltd is registered in England and Wales under company number 14106759. Privacy enquiries can be sent to info@theblacksmithmarket.com.

2. Information you provide

If you contact us by email, you may provide your name, email address, company details, product or commercial information and other information included in your message or attachments.

3. Why we use it

Information is used to respond to enquiries, assess supplier or commercial opportunities, manage correspondence, protect the website and business, and meet applicable legal obligations. The lawful basis depends on the circumstances and may include legitimate interests, steps taken before entering a contract, performance of a contract or legal obligation.

4. Supplier and commercial information

Please send only information you are authorised to share. Product files may contain business information and limited personal contact details. Sensitive personal information is not requested.

5. Website hosting and technical information

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Hosting and network providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser information, request time and requested pages to deliver and secure the service.

6. Cookies and analytics

The current website does not use an active analytics or advertising implementation. If non-essential analytics, advertising or similar technologies are introduced, this notice and any required consent controls will be updated before use.

7. Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers supporting hosting, email, professional advice or business operations where necessary. It may also be disclosed where required by law or to protect legal rights. Personal information is not sold.

8. International processing

Some technology providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Provider terms and relevant safeguards are reviewed when new services are introduced.

9. Retention

Correspondence is kept only for as long as reasonably needed for the enquiry, a potential or existing business relationship, record keeping, dispute handling and applicable legal requirements.

10. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may provide rights to access, correct, erase or restrict personal information, object to certain processing and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. Contact the email above to make a request.

11. Third-party links

The website may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by those organisations.

12. Changes

This notice will be reviewed when the website’s forms, analytics, cookies, advertising, hosting or commercial functions change.
