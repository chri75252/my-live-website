Privacy notice

How information is handled.

Effective and last updated: 27 July 2026.

Who is responsible

The controller is The Blacksmith Market Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales under company number 14106759. Privacy enquiries can be sent to info@theblacksmithmarket.com.

Information we receive

The site does not currently provide customer accounts, checkout or an on-site contact form. When you contact us by email, we may receive your name, email address, company details, message, product files and any other information you choose to provide.

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. Hosting and network providers may process technical request information such as IP address, browser information, requested URL and time of access for delivery, reliability and security. Third-party assets, including web fonts, may also receive standard request information when loaded.

Why information is used

  • To read and respond to enquiries.
  • To assess a supplier range or take requested steps before a possible business agreement.
  • To maintain website security, diagnose faults and prevent misuse.
  • To meet applicable legal, accounting or regulatory obligations.

Depending on the circumstances, the lawful basis may be legitimate interests, steps requested before entering a contract, performance of a contract or compliance with a legal obligation.

Sharing and international processing

Information may be processed by service providers needed for email, hosting, security or document handling. The public website is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses third-party web assets. Some providers may process information outside the United Kingdom under their own legal safeguards. We do not sell personal information.

Retention

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably required for the enquiry, any resulting business relationship, dispute handling and applicable legal or accounting requirements. Records that are no longer required should be deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Subject to the circumstances and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or erase information, restrict or object to processing, and complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office. A request can be sent to the contact email above. Identity verification may be required before disclosing information.

Cookies and measurement

At the date of this notice, the public source reviewed does not contain a first-party advertising, checkout or analytics tag. The site may still rely on essential hosting and third-party asset requests. This notice will be updated before materially different tracking or data collection is introduced.

Changes

This notice may be updated when the website, contact process or service providers change. The current version and modification date will remain published on this page.

This notice describes the current public website configuration. It does not replace any separate privacy terms that may apply to a later contractual business relationship.
