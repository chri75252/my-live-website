Sell to us

Share a product or stock opportunity.

Suppliers, brand owners and stock holders can send an initial range for review. Clear information makes the opportunity easier to understand.

What to include

Send enough information to identify the opportunity and assess its commercial and operational fit.

01

Company and contact

Business name, contact name, email, website and your relationship to the stock or brand.

02

Product identity

Brand, product title, SKU, EAN/GTIN, model and unit or case configuration where available.

03

Commercial terms

Unit or case pricing, VAT basis, MOQ, available quantity and any key terms.

04

Availability and documentation

Lead time, delivery location, images, specifications and relevant compliance or brand documents.

Accepted starting format

Email or spreadsheet

A clean CSV/XLSX product file is useful for larger ranges, but a concise email is sufficient for an initial single-product or small-range enquiry.

Email the opportunity

How the review works

Step 1

Information check

Confirm that the essential product, quantity and commercial information is present.

Step 2

Product and commercial review

Consider product identity, terms, route to market, operational fit and relevant documentation.

Step 3

Continue where relevant

Clarify information or continue the conversation only where a potential fit appears to exist.

Submission does not create an obligation to purchase, list, distribute or respond within a guaranteed period.
