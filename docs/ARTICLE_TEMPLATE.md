## Evidence readiness

- **First-hand basis:** What direct experience, dataset, calculation or tested workflow supports the article?
- **Primary sources:** List current official or original sources.
- **Third-party estimates:** Identify the tool, metric, date and limitation.
- **Anonymisation:** State what company, supplier or product information must be removed.
- **Material uncertainty:** Identify facts that could change the conclusion.

## Article structure

### H1 — Direct article title

Opening paragraph that answers the core question without marketing language.

### Key takeaways

- Three to five conclusions that the body demonstrates.

### Scope and definitions

Define the product, market, workflow or calculation being discussed. State what is outside scope.

### Method

Explain the sequence, required inputs, validation rules and decision criteria. Use numbered steps where order matters.

### Worked example or evidence

Include a real, anonymised or clearly illustrative example. Do not mix illustrative data with real outcomes without labelling it.

### Exceptions and failure modes

Show when the method produces an ambiguous result, needs human review or should not be used.

### Decision table/checklist

Provide a reusable framework rather than only prose.

### Limitations

State data gaps, timing, jurisdiction, marketplace, model or assumption constraints.

### Conclusion

Summarise the decision or method. Do not repeat every section.

### Sources

Use primary sources for changeable fees, rules, tax, policies and technical behaviour. Record access or publication dates where useful.

### Disclosure

State material AI assistance, commercial relationships, affiliate links or sponsorship.

## Final quality gate

- [ ] The article adds original value beyond existing search results.
- [ ] Every material claim is sourced, calculated or clearly labelled as inference.
- [ ] Current fees, laws, policies and product specifications were checked recently.
- [ ] No invented names, testimonials, statistics or business capabilities appear.
- [ ] The title, H1, description, canonical and schema agree.
- [ ] Internal and external links resolve.
- [ ] Examples preserve pack size, VAT basis, currency and units correctly.
- [ ] Limitations are sufficient for correct use.
- [ ] AI assistance, if material, is disclosed appropriately.
