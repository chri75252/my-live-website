# The Forge — twelve-month editorial roadmap

## Editorial position

**AI-assisted commerce operations, Amazon FBA sourcing, wholesale product analysis and profitability engineering.**

The sequence prioritises subjects where the site can add original methodology, examples or reusable tools. Publication dates should depend on evidence readiness rather than an arbitrary volume target.

## Core topic clusters

### 1. Product identity and listing verification

Goal: become a reliable source for the operational difference between identifier conversion and a verified commercial product match.

1. **How to Verify That a Wholesale Product Matches an Amazon ASIN**
   - Primary intent: product-match methodology.
   - Evidence needed: anonymised true-match, pack-mismatch and variation-mismatch examples.
2. **Why an Exact EAN Match Can Still Be the Wrong Amazon Product**
   - Evidence needed: cases involving multipacks, editions, regional packaging or child variations.
3. **Amazon Pack-Size Mismatches: A Decision Checklist**
   - Deliverable: downloadable checklist.
4. **EAN, GTIN, UPC, ASIN and Model Numbers: Which Signal Wins?**
   - Deliverable: evidence hierarchy and conflict-resolution table.

### 2. FBA unit economics and capital exposure

Goal: explain true commercial viability rather than provide a superficial fee estimate.

5. **The Real UK Amazon FBA Net-Profit Formula**
   - Evidence needed: current official Amazon and HMRC sources plus an anonymised worked example.
6. **MOQ Profitability: Why Positive Unit Margin Can Still Be a Bad Deal**
   - Deliverable: cash-exposure and sensitivity model.
7. **How Pack Size, VAT and Prep Costs Change FBA ROI**
   - Evidence needed: controlled scenarios using one product.
8. **Storage, Returns and Price Decline: Building a Downside Case**
   - Deliverable: conservative, expected and optimistic scenarios.

### 3. Supplier and catalogue analysis

Goal: turn supplier research into an auditable procurement workflow.

9. **A Scoring Model for Prioritising UK Wholesalers**
   - Deliverable: scorecard with legitimacy, catalogue quality, data matchability, commercial effort and operational fit.
10. **From Supplier CSV to Amazon Product Shortlist**
    - Evidence needed: anonymised input/output and exception register.
11. **How to Compare the Same Product Across Multiple Suppliers**
    - Deliverable: price-history and second-lowest-supplier model.
12. **Supplier Catalogue Data Quality: The Fields That Prevent Expensive Errors**
    - Deliverable: supplier data specification.

### 4. Automation, agents and report assurance

Goal: distinguish dependable automation from attractive but unauditable AI output.

13. **Deterministic Automation vs AI Agents for FBA Research**
    - Evidence needed: tasks allocated to rules, search, models and human review.
14. **How to Audit an AI-Generated Product Research Report**
    - Deliverable: quality-control checklist.
15. **Designing Confidence Levels for Product Matching**
    - Deliverable: confirmed, probable, ambiguous and rejected definitions.
16. **Building an Audit Trail for Ecommerce Research Workflows**
    - Deliverable: reproducibility and exception-log template.

### 5. Search and AI discovery — secondary cluster

Goal: document practical experiments on the site itself rather than become a generic SEO publication.

17. **Search and AI-Discovery Checklist for Small-Business Websites** — published foundation guide.
18. **What Changed After Correcting Canonicals, Sitemap and Trust Signals**
    - Evidence needed: before/after Search Console data after a sufficient observation period.
19. **How Google AI Mode Sources Product-Research Answers**
    - Evidence needed: dated query sample and source classification.
20. **Is `llms.txt` Useful? A Measured Small-Site Test**
    - Evidence needed: crawl logs, citations or clearly stated inability to measure causality.

## Article acceptance gate

An article should not be published unless it has:

- One clear intent and canonical URL
- Accurate publisher or author attribution
- Original examples, data, methodology or tested workflow
- Current primary sources for changeable external facts
- Stated assumptions and limitations
- A reusable table, checklist, calculator or decision model where suitable
- Internal links to its topic hub and related material
- Valid `BlogPosting` structured data
- A real publication and modification date

## Topics not to prioritise

- Generic “What is AI?” or “What is SEO?” articles
- Broad “How to start Amazon FBA” guides
- Unsourced “best products to sell” lists
- Generic AI-tool roundups
- Automatically generated industry news summaries
- “Best UK wholesalers” lists without documented methodology and current verification

## Future commercial paths

The content can later support:

- Downloadable analytical templates
- Product-match review services
- Supplier catalogue audits
- FBA profitability modelling
- Workflow implementation or consulting
- A product-research or reporting tool

No commercial offering should be implied on the website until its scope, delivery process and terms are real.
